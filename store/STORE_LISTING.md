### Description
```
Klopatra is the bathroom-tracking app with a wink — but serious where it counts. Log your visits in seconds, see your patterns at a glance and let Klopatra predict your next likely session. Rule your throne! 👑🚽

WHAT KLOPATRA DOES
• Quick log: bowel movement or urination in one tap
• Bristol scale in plain language (hard, ideal, liquid) with colour zones
• Drink tracking with a daily goal — hydration helps digestion
• "Time on the throne" timer — plus your weekly sitting total 😄
• Health check with honest, light-hearted hints
• Symptom notes (e.g. blood, pain) with a factual warning
• Stats: 24-hour rhythm, weekly overview, trends & patterns
• Achievements to keep you going
• Compare with friends via code/link — no account needed

PRIVACY FIRST
All data stays exclusively on your device. No server, no account, no tracking, no ads. Nobody sees your entries but you.

KLOPATRA PRO
Try everything free for 4 days. Then unlock the smart extras for good with Pro:
• Next-visit prediction
• Daily-rhythm analysis
• Trends & patterns
• Doctor report as a printable PDF
If you like, leave a "tip for the toilet paper" 🧻 — optional, anytime.

IMPORTANT
Klopatra is not a medical app and does not replace professional advice, diagnosis or treatment. For persistent symptoms, blood in stool or severe pain, please consult a doctor.

Made with 💛 and a roll of toilet paper.
```
